# T-11.4 – Daily attendance and By employee tabs

**Goal:** on All Attendance, Admin and Staff see in a "Daily attendance" tab every employee for one date as Present or Absent, and in a "By employee" tab one chosen employee's attendance day by day over a date range.

**Depends on:** T-11, T-11.1

**Current implementation**

- `/attendance` (route `attendance.index`, permission `attendance.view-all`, menu item in `App\Support\Navigation::items()`) is `App\Livewire\Attendance\Index` with view `resources/views/livewire/attendance/index.blade.php`:
  - `#[Url]` filters `from`, `to` (default this week through `AttendanceHistoryService::range()` / `currentWeek()`), `user`, `project`, `status`; `clearDates()` and `clearFilters()`.
  - Stat cards: Attendance records, Total hours, Labor cost, Clocked In now.
  - One table of attendance records (Date, Name, Project, In, Out, Worked time, Rate, Labor cost, Photos, Status) with a Total row (labor cost) and phone cards, paginated with `WithPerPage` and `<x-table-footer>`.
- Reused pieces: `AttendanceHistoryService::all()`, `totals()`, `clockedInNow()`; partials `livewire.attendance.partials.clock-out-time` (In/Out with `+1` superscript), `worked-time` (running time while Clocked In, otherwise `4.50 h`), `photo-thumbs` (thumbnails and the `attendance-photo` modal), `<x-avatar>`, `<x-status-badge>`, `App\Support\Money::format()`.
- The Status column shows the attendance status (`Attendance::STATUSES`: Clocked In, Clocked Out, Adjusted, Flagged, Void). This ticket replaces it with **Present / Absent** on both tabs.
- There are no attendance rows for days someone did not work, so "Absent" has to be built from the list of employees, not from `attendances`.
- `User` has `first_name`, `last_name`, `name`, `employee_no`, `status` (`active` / `inactive`), `SoftDeletes` and roles; every role has `attendance.clock`.
- Flux free has no tabs component; the tabs are a small custom tab bar built with brand tokens.

**Build**

- Presence rule (in `App\Models\Attendance`): `PRESENT_STATUSES = [open, closed, adjusted, flagged]` (everything except `void`). An employee is **Present** on a date when they have at least one attendance with that `work_date` in `PRESENT_STATUSES`; otherwise **Absent** (an employee whose only attendance that day is void is Absent). Void attendance is not listed in either tab.
- Presence badge partial `resources/views/livewire/attendance/partials/presence-badge.blade.php`: Present `<x-status-badge color="green">`, Absent `<x-status-badge color="zinc">` (the same gray as Clocked Out), `data-test="presence-present"` / `presence-absent`.
- `AttendanceHistoryService` (new methods; the old single-table `all()`, `totals()`, `filtered()` and `clockedInNow()` are removed because nothing uses them any more):
  - `employees(): Builder<User>` — everyone who can clock in (`User::permission('attendance.clock')`), active and not archived, ordered by `last_name`, then `first_name`.
  - `daily(string $date, ?int $userId, int $perPage): LengthAwarePaginator` — one item per employee, ordered by last name (paginated by employee): `['user' => User, 'present' => bool, 'attendances' => EloquentCollection<Attendance>]` with that date's non-void attendance (project loaded, ordered by `clock_in_at`). Employees who are inactive or archived but have attendance on that date are included too, so no record is hidden. `$userId` narrows it to one employee.
  - `dailySummary(string $date, ?int $userId): array{employees: int, present: int, absent: int, labor_cost: string}` — counts with SQL, labor cost as the sum of `labor_cost` of counted attendance (closed, adjusted) as in `totals()`.
  - `employeeDays(int $userId, string $from, string $to, int $perPage): LengthAwarePaginator` — **one item per date** in the range, newest first, paginated by day: `['date' => Carbon, 'present' => bool, 'attendances' => EloquentCollection<Attendance>]`; dates without attendance are Absent. The range is capped at 92 days (a longer range is shortened to 92 days ending at `to`, with a note under the filters).
  - `employeeSummary(int $userId, string $from, string $to): array{present_days: int, absent_days: int, hours: string, labor_cost: string}` — hours and labor cost summed from counted attendance in SQL.
- `App\Livewire\Attendance\Index` becomes a page with two tabs, kept in the URL as `?tab=daily` (default) or `?tab=employee`:
  - New properties: `#[Url(as: 'tab')] public string $tab = 'daily'` (only `daily` / `employee` accepted, anything else falls back to `daily`), `#[Url] public string $date = ''` (default today in `Asia/Manila`; an invalid date falls back to today), `#[Url(as: 'employee')] public string $employee = ''` for the By employee tab. `from`, `to` and `user` stay. The `project` and `status` filters and their chips are removed.
  - `boot()` keeps checking `attendance.view-all`; switching tabs resets the page number; `clearFilters()` resets the current tab's filters to their defaults.
- Tab bar under the page header: two tabs with icons, "Daily attendance" (`calendar-days`) and "By employee" (`user`), `role="tablist"`, the active tab with a `brand-gold` underline and `aria-selected="true"`, `data-test="tab-daily"` / `tab-employee`; on phones the two tabs share the width.
- **Daily attendance** tab:
  - Filters: Date (`flux:input type="date"`, default today) and Employee (`flux:select`, first option "All employees", default All; the list shows "Last, First (EMP-0042)" ordered by last name), `<x-filter-chip>`s and "Clear filters" when not on today / All.
  - Stat cards: Employees, Present (green), Absent (gray), Labor cost for that date.
  - Table: Name (`<x-avatar>` + "Last, First"), Project, In, Out, Worked time, Rate, Labor cost, Photos, Status (Present / Absent). An employee with several attendance records that day (e.g. morning and afternoon) gets one row per record, with the name and Status on the first row only; an Absent employee gets a single row with dashes and the gray "Absent" badge. Ordered by last name ascending. Total row: labor cost for the date. Phones: one card per employee with the presence badge and their attendance under it.
  - Empty state when the employee filter matches nobody: "No employees to show."
- **By employee** tab:
  - Filters: Employee (`flux:select` with the placeholder "Select employee", default none) and From / To dates (default this week, same as today's page).
  - With no employee selected: the table is not shown; an `<x-empty-state>` reads "Select an employee to see their attendance." (`data-test="choose-employee"`).
  - With an employee: stat cards Present days, Absent days, Total hours, Labor cost; table Date, Project, In, Out, Worked time, Rate, Labor cost, Photos, Status — one row per attendance on days with attendance (date and Status on the first row of the day), one gray "Absent" row with dashes on days without; newest date first; Total row with hours and labor cost for the range. Phones: one card per day.
- **My Attendance** (`/attendance/mine`, `App\Livewire\Attendance\Mine`) shows the same Present / Absent days for the signed-in user through `employeeDays()` and `employeeSummary()`: every day of the range newest first, Absent rows with dashes, stat cards Present days, Absent days and Total hours, the Total row with hours, the 92-day note; still no peso amounts and no photos. The old `ownDays()` / `ownTotals()` are removed.
- Both tables keep the existing partials (`clock-out-time`, `worked-time` with the running time while Clocked In, `photo-thumbs`), `WithPerPage` with `<x-table-footer>`, stacked cards on phones, and never show the word "session".
- Update `tests/Feature/Attendance/AllAttendanceTest.php` where it expects the old single table, the `project` / `status` filters or the Clocked In / Clocked Out badges on this page; other pages keep those badges.

**Acceptance criteria**

- [ ] Opening All Attendance shows the "Daily attendance" tab for today with every active employee, ordered by last name, each marked Present (green) or Absent (gray).
- [ ] An employee who clocked in at 7:00 AM and out at 11:30 AM today shows that row (project, 7:00 AM, 11:30 AM, 4.50 h, rate, ₱ 360.00) with Present; an employee with no attendance today shows one row with dashes and Absent; an employee whose only attendance is void shows Absent.
- [ ] Picking another date shows that date's presence; picking one employee shows only them.
- [ ] The "By employee" tab shows "Select an employee to see their attendance." until an employee is chosen; after choosing one with attendance on Monday and Wednesday of this week, the table lists every day of the range with Monday and Wednesday Present and the other days Absent.
- [ ] Present days, Absent days, hours and labor cost on the By employee tab match the rows.
- [ ] The tab, date and employee stay in the URL, so a reload or a shared link opens the same view.
- [ ] Staff see both tabs; Workers still get 403 on `/attendance`.
