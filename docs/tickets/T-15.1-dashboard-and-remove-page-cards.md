# T-15.1 – Dashboard and remove page cards

**Goal:** everyone lands on a new Dashboard at the top of the menu: Admin sees today's key numbers and Today's Attendance for every employee; Staff and Workers see their own recent attendance with a Clock In / Clock Out shortcut. The menu pages lose their rows of stat cards but keep their description, filters and tables.

**Depends on:** T-15 (Material Entries page, adds cards this ticket removes), T-13 (flagged count), T-11.4 (Daily attendance list), T-11.1 (devices)

**Current implementation**

- After login everyone lands on Clock In/Out:
  - `routes/web.php` sends `/` to `route('clock')`
  - `config/fortify.php` has `'home' => '/clock'`
  - The sidebar logo links to `route('clock')` (`resources/views/layouts/app/sidebar.blade.php` line 9)
  - There is no dashboard; `NavigationTest::testTheStarterKitDashboardIsGone` asserts `/dashboard` is 404.
- The menu comes from `App\Support\Navigation::items()`, grouped by `group` ("Attendance", "Operations", "Administration"). The sidebar renders one `flux:sidebar.group` with a heading per group (`groupedFor()`), and `routes/web.php` registers one route per item with `Navigation::middlewareFor()`.
- Rows of `<x-stat-card>` on menu pages, each fed by a computed property:
  - All Attendance, Daily attendance tab (`resources/views/livewire/attendance/partials/daily-tab.blade.php` lines 5–8): Employees, Present, Absent, Labor cost, from `Attendance\Index::dailySummary()` → `AttendanceHistoryService::dailySummary()`.
  - All Attendance, By employee tab (`partials/employee-tab.blade.php` lines 63–66): Present days, Absent days, Total hours, from `Attendance\Index::employeeSummary()` → `AttendanceHistoryService::employeeSummary()`.
  - My Attendance (`resources/views/livewire/attendance/mine.blade.php` lines 6–10): Present days, Absent days, Total hours, from `Attendance\Mine::totals()`.
  - Projects (`projects/index.blade.php`): Total, Ongoing, On hold, Completed, from `Projects\Index::summary()`.
  - Materials (`materials/index.blade.php`): Total, Active, Inactive, from `Materials\Index::summary()`.
  - Users (`users/index.blade.php`): Total, Active, Inactive, Workers vs admins, from `Users\Index::summary()`.
  - Devices (`devices/index.blade.php`): Pending requests, Approved, Blocked, from `Devices\Index::summary()`.
  - Material Entries (T-15, `material-entries/index.blade.php`): Entries, Markup, Total cost, from `MaterialEntries\Index::totals()`. The same `totals` also feed the table's totals row.
- The Import Attendance result cards (Rows read, Imported, Not imported in `attendance/import.blade.php`) describe one upload, not the page. They stay.
- Reusable pieces:
  - `AttendanceHistoryService::daily($date, $userId, $projectId, $sortBy, $perPage)`: one row per employee on a date, by last name, Present / Absent / Rest Day, absent employees included
  - `AttendanceHistoryService::employeeDays($userId, $from, $to, $perPage)`: one row per date, newest first
  - `AttendanceHistoryService::dailySummary()` and `flaggedCount()`
  - `AttendanceService::openSessionFor()` and `flaggedFor()`
  - `App\Support\AttendanceDay` and the row partials `day-cells`, `day-worked`, `day-remarks`, `presence-badge`
  - `flagged-notice`, `<x-stat-card>`, `<x-data-card>`, `<x-empty-state>`, `<x-table-footer>`, `WithPerPage`
- Device statuses: `Device::STATUS_PENDING`.
- CLAUDE.md and `docs/TICKETS.md` ("Page design") still say "list pages add a row of `<x-stat-card>`s". T-20 (not built) plans three role cards.

**Build**

- New permission `dashboard.overview`, Admin only, in `RolesAndPermissionsSeeder::ROLE_PERMISSIONS['admin']`. It decides which dashboard a user sees.
- `App\Services\DashboardService`:
  - `overview(): array{clocked_in: int, present: int, employees: int, needs_correction: int, pending_devices: int}` for today (Asia/Manila):
    - `clocked_in`: users with an `open` attendance now
    - `present` and `employees`: from `AttendanceHistoryService::dailySummary(today, null, null)`
    - `needs_correction`: `AttendanceHistoryService::flaggedCount()`
    - `pending_devices`: devices with `status = pending`, not archived
  - `todaysAttendance(int $perPage)`: `AttendanceHistoryService::daily(today, null, null, 'name', $perPage)`, every employee by last name, including those not clocked in yet.
  - `myRecentDays(User $user, int $perPage)`: `AttendanceHistoryService::employeeDays($user->id, today − 13 days, today, $perPage)`. That is the last 14 days, newest first, today on top.
- Livewire `App\Livewire\Dashboard` (class in `app/Livewire/Dashboard.php`, view in `resources/views/livewire/dashboard.blade.php`), `#[Title('Dashboard')]`, `use WithPerPage`:
  - `boot()`: `abort_unless(Auth::user()?->can('attendance.view-own'), 403)`.
  - Users with `dashboard.overview` see the **Admin dashboard**:
    - `<x-page-header>` "Dashboard", with the description "Today at a glance, :date." (e.g. "Thursday, Nov 19, 2026").
    - A row of four `<x-stat-card>`s, each a link:
      - "Clocked in now" (value `clocked_in`, note "Working right now") → `/attendance`
      - "Present today" (value ":present of :employees") → `/attendance`
      - "Needs correction" (amber when above 0, note "Not clocked out") → `/attendance?needs_correction=1`
      - "Pending device requests" → `/devices?status=pending`
    - `<x-data-card>` titled "Today's Attendance", with no filters:
      - The same rows as the Daily attendance tab: Name with avatar, Morning, Afternoon, Worked time, Status (Present / Absent / Rest Day), Remarks.
      - Read-only, with no Correct menu.
      - Stacked cards on phones, `<x-table-footer>` with rows per page (10 / 25 / 50 / 100, default 10).
      - A "Open All Attendance" link in the card header.
    - Extract the Daily tab's table rows into one partial (`resources/views/livewire/attendance/partials/daily-table.blade.php`) used by both pages. The Daily tab keeps its Correct menu through a `correctable` flag.
  - Everyone else sees the **My dashboard** (Staff and Worker):
    - `<x-page-header>` "Dashboard", with the description "Your attendance for the last 14 days."
    - The `flagged-notice` partial (T-13).
    - A clock card:
      - When Clocked In: "Clocked In since 7:02 AM · {project}" with a large "Clock Out" button.
      - Otherwise: "Not clocked in" with a large "Clock In" button (`btn-accent`).
      - Both buttons link to `/clock`, shown only with `attendance.clock`.
    - `<x-data-card>` titled "My Attendance", with no filters:
      - `myRecentDays()` rows with the My Attendance columns: Date, Morning, Afternoon, Worked time, Status, Remarks. Hours only, no peso amounts and no photos.
      - Newest first, stacked cards on phones, `<x-table-footer>`.
      - A "See all in My Attendance" link.
- Menu and landing:
  - Add the first item to `Navigation::items()`: `['label' => 'Dashboard', 'url' => 'dashboard', 'route' => 'dashboard', 'group' => '', 'icon' => 'home', 'permissions' => ['attendance.view-own'], 'component' => Dashboard::class]`.
  - Items with `group => ''` render first, with no group heading (`sidebar.blade.php`). `Navigation::breadcrumbs()` leaves out the empty group crumb.
  - `/` sends signed-in users to `route('dashboard')`. Set `config/fortify.php` `'home' => '/dashboard'`. The sidebar logo links to `route('dashboard')`. The topbar Clock In/Out button stays.
- Remove the stat-card rows, keeping each page's header, description, filters, chips and table:
  - Daily attendance and By employee tabs: delete `Attendance\Index::dailySummary()` and `employeeSummary()`.
  - My Attendance: delete `Mine::totals()`.
  - Projects, Materials, Users, Devices: delete each component's `summary()`.
  - Material Entries: remove the three cards; keep `totals()` for the totals row.
  - Keep `AttendanceHistoryService::dailySummary()` (the dashboard uses it) and `employeeSummary()` (T-18 reports).
- Update the design rule:
  - In CLAUDE.md and the "Project conventions" in `docs/TICKETS.md`, change "list pages add a row of `<x-stat-card>`s, a filter toolbar card …" to "list pages add a filter toolbar card … (stat cards only on the Dashboard)".
  - In `docs/tickets/T-20-roles-and-permissions-screen.md`, drop its row of three stat cards. Add `dashboard.overview` "See the admin dashboard" (Administration) to its permission list.
- Wording: never "session" or "duty". Use "Clocked In", "Clocked Out", "attendance", and the statuses Present / Absent / Rest Day.

**Acceptance criteria**

- [ ] After logging in, Admin, Staff and Worker land on `/dashboard`. "Dashboard" is the first menu item, above the Attendance group, with no group heading.
- [ ] Admin's dashboard shows four cards with today's numbers:
  - Clocked in now 2
  - Present today "3 of 5"
  - Needs correction 1
  - Pending device requests 1

  This is for 5 active employees, 3 with attendance today (2 still Clocked In), 1 flagged attendance and 1 pending device. Each card opens its page.
- [ ] "Today's Attendance" lists all 5 employees by last name, including the 2 who have not clocked in (Absent, or Rest Day on their rest day), with the same Morning / Afternoon / Worked time as the Daily attendance tab, paginated 10 per page.
- [ ] A Worker's dashboard shows only their own attendance for the last 14 days, newest first (today on top), with no other employee's name. A "Clock In" button shows when not clocked in; "Clocked In since …" and a "Clock Out" button show when clocked in.
- [ ] Staff see the same personal dashboard as Workers, not the admin cards. A Staff user changing nothing in the URL cannot reach the admin numbers.
- [ ] No `<x-stat-card>` remains on All Attendance (both tabs), My Attendance, Projects, Materials, Users, Devices or Material Entries. Their descriptions, filters, chips and tables still work. The Import Attendance result cards still show after an upload.
- [ ] Nothing on screen says "session".
